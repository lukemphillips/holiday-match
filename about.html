About us

Family holidays, made easier.

Holiday Match helps families travelling from Ireland and the UK discover holiday destinations that fit their travel dates, budget, children’s age groups and preferred style of trip.

How it works

  1. Answer a few short questions about your family and the trip you have in mind.
  2. Explore a ranked shortlist of destination ideas, with accommodation suggestions and family activity inspiration.
  3. Use the links to research travel, stays and activities directly with third-party websites.

What to know

Holiday Match is a planning and discovery tool, not a travel agent or booking service. Recommendations are generated from general destination information and your answers. They are not personalised professional travel advice, and we do not check live fares, accommodation availability or current prices.

Budget ranges are broad estimates to help with early planning. Actual costs depend on your departure point, dates, family size, accommodation, transport and choices made. Always verify details with providers before booking.

We aim to make it easier to compare possibilities without pretending there is one perfect holiday for every family.
