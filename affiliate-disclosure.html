Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

Current position: Holiday Match does not currently use active affiliate booking links. The travel, accommodation and activity buttons on this site open general search results and do not currently earn us a commission.

If we introduce affiliate links

Holiday Match may join travel affiliate programmes in the future. If a link becomes an affiliate link, we may earn a commission when you make an eligible booking after clicking it. This would normally be at no additional cost to you, although the provider’s own prices and terms apply.

We will clearly identify commercial links or relevant content so you can recognise when Holiday Match may benefit from a booking. A commission would not guarantee that a destination or provider is the best choice for your family.

Prices and bookings

Holiday Match does not currently take bookings, sell travel or display live prices. Search results and third-party offers may change. Always review the provider’s final price, cancellation conditions, baggage rules, suitability and booking terms before paying.
