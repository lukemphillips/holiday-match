Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how the Holiday Match website handles information when you visit and use the holiday finder. The site is an early-stage, static website hosted using GitHub Pages.

Answers you enter into the holiday finder

Your answers and optional preference notes are used by the page’s JavaScript in your browser to rank destination ideas. The current version does not send those answers to a Holiday Match server, create an account or store a user profile for us to access. If you leave or refresh the page, the current shortlist may be lost.

Technical information and hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as connection data and server logs as part of operating and securing its service. Please refer to GitHub’s Privacy Statement for information about its processing.

External websites and images

The website loads some photographs from Unsplash and offers links to Google search results. If you load external images or follow an external link, those third parties may receive technical information from your browser and apply their own privacy policies and technologies. We do not control their sites or their data practices.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current version of Holiday Match does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include an analytics service. If we add analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking or other technologies that require consent, we will update this policy and provide appropriate consent controls before using them where required by law.

Children

Holiday Match is intended for adults planning family travel. It does not ask for children’s names or other direct identifiers. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in the optional notes field.

Your choices and contact

You can use the holiday finder without entering a name, email address or account details. For privacy questions, please raise an issue through the Holiday Match GitHub repository. Do not include private or sensitive information in a public issue.

This policy describes the current implementation and may need updating as the website gains analytics, affiliate links, contact forms or other features. It is provided as a practical privacy notice, not legal advice.
